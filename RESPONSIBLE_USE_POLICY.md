# Responsible Use Policy

Use Web3Guard AI only for projects you own or are authorized to review.

## Allowed
- Scan your own Solidity code.
- Run passive website checks for your own or authorized project.
- Use checklists to prepare your dApp/API/wallet/admin setup.
- Request paid review with clear scope and authorization.

## Not allowed
- Unauthorized scanning of third-party systems.
- Brute force, credential testing, exploit automation, or destructive testing.
- Using results to attack, extort, or harass projects.
- Submitting private keys, seed phrases, stolen code, or secrets.

Deep scans must require ownership verification and written scope in a future production system.

---

## Phase 5.9 Ownership Proof

Users should verify ownership before any future deeper website/API/repo-connected workflow. Supported proof methods are DNS TXT and `.well-known` file token verification.

Ownership verification does not mean Web3Guard AI by RAADHANEX endorses, certifies, audits, or guarantees the project.

The platform must reject or avoid requests involving third-party unauthorized scanning, credential testing, exploit automation, or destructive activity.
